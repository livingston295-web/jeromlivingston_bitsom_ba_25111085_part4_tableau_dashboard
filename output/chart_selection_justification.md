# Chart Selection Justification

## 1. Sales Trend View

### Business Question

How are sales changing over time?

### Chart Type

Line Chart

### Why This Chart?

A line chart is the best choice for showing trends and patterns over time.

### Fields Used

* X-Axis: Order Date
* Y-Axis: Sales

### Design Principle Applied

Simple trend visualization with minimal clutter.

### Mistake Avoided

Avoided using pie charts for time-series data.

---

## 2. Regional Performance View

### Business Question

Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?

### Chart Type

Horizontal Bar Chart

### Why This Chart?

Bar charts make regional comparisons easy and readable.

### Fields Used

* Region
* Sales
* Profit

### Design Principle Applied

Sorted values to highlight top-performing regions.

### Mistake Avoided

Avoided unnecessary 3D charts.
